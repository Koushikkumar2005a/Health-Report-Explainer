About This Tool

Health Report Explainer is an AI-powered tool designed to help you understand the contents of your medical and laboratory reports. It reads your report, identifies the tests and their results, and explains them in simple, everyday language.

This tool does not diagnose you or replace your doctor. It simply helps you better understand the information in your report, so you can have more informed conversations with your healthcare provider.

⚕️

Medical Disclaimer: This tool provides an AI-generated explanation of the information contained in your report. It is for educational and informational purposes only and is not a medical diagnosis or a substitute for professional medical advice. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for diagnosis and treatment. If you are experiencing severe or emergency symptoms, seek immediate medical care.

🔒

Privacy: Uploaded medical reports may contain sensitive personal information. We follow a privacy-first approach: reports are processed for analysis and are not permanently stored. Temporary files are deleted after processing. Your patient information is not displayed unnecessarily.
